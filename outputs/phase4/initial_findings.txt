AMAZON CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE
PHASE 4: INITIAL EDA FINDINGS
==================================================

Dataset scope
- Reviews analyzed: 5,000
- Products represented: 4,509
- The sample consists of the first 5,000 source records.
- Findings describe the available sample, not all Amazon customers.

Rating distribution
- Average rating: 4.27/5.
- Reviews rated 1 or 2 stars: 11.82%.
- Five-star reviews represent 65.42% of valid ratings.

Review volume over time
- The highest-volume month in this sample is 2019-12 (103 reviews).
- Monthly counts describe the sample's recorded dates; they do not necessarily measure actual platform-wide volume.

Verified purchase comparison
- Unverified: 1,650 reviews; average rating 4.36; 1-2 star rate 6.48%.
- Verified: 3,350 reviews; average rating 4.23; 1-2 star rate 14.45%.
- Differences are descriptive associations, not evidence that verification status causes a rating difference.

Product comparisons
- No products met the threshold of 20 sampled reviews.

Sampling limitations
- The dataset contains the first 5,000 source records. Record order may affect which reviews are included.
- The results describe this sample and should not automatically be generalized to all Amazon Electronics reviews.
- The five most-represented products account for 1.36% of all rows. Product concentration may cause the overall results to reflect a small number of products.
- The first half has an average rating of 4.28; the second half has 4.27. A difference may indicate ordering effects, but does not prove sampling bias.
- Median review dates by source order: first half = 2019-12-22 00:13:20.131500032, second half = 2019-08-17 13:02:53.401500160. If these differ substantially, the source order may also be time-related.
- A first-record sample cannot establish population representativeness without knowing the source ordering and the population sampling process.

Interpretation cautions
- Rating and review patterns do not establish causation.
- Helpful votes measure observed engagement, not necessarily quality.
- These initial summaries do not identify the causes of dissatisfaction.
- Further text/theme analysis is needed before recommending specific fixes.
